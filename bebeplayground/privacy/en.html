This app is a sound play app for young children. We care about the privacy of children and their families. This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Personal information we collect

  • The app does not collect any personal information, such as names, contact details, location, photos or voice recordings, and does not send any data to our servers.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app does not use analytics tools.

2. Information stored on this device

Settings such as the language, showing English names, reading names aloud, sound effects volume and the parent lock type are stored only on this device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Content

Photos, videos and sounds are either included in the app or downloaded through Google Play and stored on this device. Downloads are handled by Google Play; we do not run our own content servers.

4. Reading names aloud (text-to-speech)

To read names aloud, the app uses the text-to-speech (TTS) engine installed on this device. The app only passes the names to be read to that engine; it does not record, store or send any voice data.

The TTS engine is provided by your device or the engine's developer, and its own policy applies to how it works.

5. Ads

The app shows Google AdMob banner ads only at the bottom of the home screen and of the item detail screen (in portrait orientation).

  • Every ad request is tagged as child-directed (TFCD), the maximum ad content rating is set to G (general audiences), and only non-personalized ads are shown.
  • The app removes the advertising ID (AD_ID) permission and does not use the advertising ID.

Google AdMob may still process device information, such as the IP address, to serve ads and to prevent fraud.

For details, see the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

6. In-app purchases

This version of the app has no in-app purchases, and the app does not handle any payment information.

7. Children

The app is designed for young children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Grown-up features such as settings are available only after a parent check.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact (email) shown on the app's Google Play Store page.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be provided through an app update.
